# **Freelook**

#### Allows you to look around in third person without changing your serverside view.

---

## Mode

### Hold

Only activates when you are currently holding down your bind.

### Toggle

Toggles activation when pressing your bind.

## Start position

### Forward

Makes your view look forward when enabling freelook.

### Backwards

Makes your view look backwards when enabling freelook.

## Custom fov

Changes the FOV while using freelook, acts as way to customize the distance.

## Bind

The bind that enables freelook while held.
