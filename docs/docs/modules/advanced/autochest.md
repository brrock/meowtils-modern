# **AutoChest**

#### Automatically deposits resources into your Bedwars chest.

---

## Delay

Click delay for depositing items.

!!! Warning

    Low delays may not act naturally and could be detected on stricter anticheats.

## Wait delay

Wait delay until this starts depositing items into the chest.

## Dump

When clicked while inside of an allowed chest, this will dump all items in your inventory including gear.

## Take

When clicked, this will take out all resources from an allowed chest.

## Render clicked

Renders a background for the slot clicked when depositing items.

## Allow for Enderchest

When enabled this will activate for enderchests.

## Allow for Chest

When enabled this will activate for normal chests (team chests).

## Items

Enable which resources this should activate for.

- Iron Ingots
- Gold Ingots
- Diamonds
- Emeralds
